# Staff Leave Policy

## Annual leave

Permanent staff accrue 1.25 days of annual leave per month, which is 15 working days per year. Leave requests must be submitted in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance.

## Sick leave

Staff are entitled to 30 days of paid sick leave over a three-year cycle. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than two consecutive days.

## Salary bands

Salary bands are reviewed every March. Band details are confidential to HR and line managers.

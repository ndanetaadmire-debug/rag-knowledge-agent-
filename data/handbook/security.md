# Information Security Standard

## Passwords and access

All staff accounts require multi-factor authentication. Passwords must be at least 14 characters and must never be shared, including with IT support.

## Customer data

Customer personal information is processed under POPIA. Exports of customer data require written approval from the information officer and must be encrypted at rest.

## Incident reporting

Suspected security incidents must be reported to security@company.example within one hour of discovery.

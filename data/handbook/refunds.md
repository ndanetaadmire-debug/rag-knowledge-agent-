# Refunds and Returns Policy

## Eligibility

Customers may return unused items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be in their original packaging with tags attached. Wigs that have been worn, cut or coloured cannot be returned for hygiene reasons.

## Damaged items

If an item arrives damaged, the customer must report it within 7 days and include photos. We replace or refund damaged items at no cost, including return shipping. Refunds above R2,000 require approval from the operations manager.

## Processing times

Approved refunds are paid back to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days. EFT refunds need the customer's verified bank details.

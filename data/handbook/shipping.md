# Shipping Guide

## Delivery times

Orders placed before 14:00 on a business day are dispatched the same day. Gauteng deliveries take 1 to 2 business days; other main centres take 2 to 3 business days; outlying areas take 3 to 5 business days.

## Costs

Standard delivery costs R99. Orders over R750 ship free within South Africa. We do not currently ship internationally.

## Tracking

Every order gets a tracking number from The Courier Guy by email once it is dispatched.
